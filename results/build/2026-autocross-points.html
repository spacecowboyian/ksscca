How points work. Class points go by finishing position in class: 100, 80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10, 5, then 1 for every place after that. PAX points start at 100 for the top PAX time of the day and drop by one per place. A driver with no time scores nothing. Every event counts so far; once the season is long enough, the lowest scores are dropped. Final standings for past seasons are in the results archive.

# Trophy spots at Kansas Region autocross

Worked out on 2026-09-29 from the T markers on our own published results,
because no written rule covers per-event trophies (see "What the rules say"
below). Recorded to apply to future event pages; **not yet applied to any
page**, since our pages have never shown trophy marks.

## The house rule

For a class at a regular Kansas Region event:

    spots = ceil(2 x timed entries / 3), never more than 6

Entries here means drivers who set a time; a driver with no time is not counted
and gets no spot.

| Timed entries | Spots | | Timed entries | Spots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | | 6 | 4 |
| 2 | 2 | | 7 | 5 |
| 3 | 2 | | 8 | 6 |
| 4 | 3 | | 9 or more | 6 |
| 5 | 4 | | | |

## Divisional and other special events

    spots = ceil(timed entries / 3), and a class of one gets none

Seen on the 2024 MiDiv weekend and the 2023 joint event: the same shape as the
national default, and the one kind of event the region's own regulations allow
to carry trophies.

## The evidence

Each event's markers were fitted against six candidate rules; the best fit:

| Event | Best fit | Classes matching |
|---|---|---|
| April 9, 2022 | ceil(2n/3) cap 6 | 20 of 22 |
| May 14, 2022 | ceil(2n/3) cap 6 | 31 of 33 |
| June 8, 2024 (Event 3) | ceil(2n/3) cap 6 | 13 of 14 |
| June 9, 2024 (Event 4) | ceil(2n/3) cap 6 | 7 of 12 |
| June 27, 2026 (Come Back Tour) | ceil(2n/3) cap 6 | 39 of 40 |
| May 4, 2024 (MiDiv day 1) | ceil(n/3), none for a class of 1 | 22 of 25 |
| July 1, 2023 (joint event) | ceil(n/3), none for a class of 1 | 9 of 13 |

The misfits are single classes here and there, not a competing pattern. The
count that matters is timed entries: counting entrants including non-starters
fits noticeably worse.

## What the rules say

The region's supplemental regulations (2018, linked from the Autocross
Supplemental Rules page) are about year-end awards, and section 8.L says
outright: "There will be no individual event trophies or awards with the
exception of special events such as the Midwest Divisional Solo Championship."

So the T markers on the timing sheets are the timing system's own setting
rather than an entitlement. Year-end, per the same regulations: class trophies
to the top 25% of qualifying drivers or the top 3, whichever is greater
(section 8.F); PAX trophies to the top 10 (8.K).

## The timing site does something else again

ksscca.prontolivetiming.com marks `ceil(entries / 3)`, one spot per three
entrants, which is the national default and matches neither rule above. For
September 19 that came to 36 marks where the house rule gives 51.
